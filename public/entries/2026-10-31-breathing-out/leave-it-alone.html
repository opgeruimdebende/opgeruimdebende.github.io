Clears 0 / 15 Tap Clear to start the board.

A stand-in for a Wa board: every clear plays a note, and every third clear moves the chord. Stop clearing and the chord holds, then breathes out. Clear again and it wakes, a little softer. From 60% of the board a faint high layer joins. Built from the game's own breath files, a new breath every three seconds, each set a little quieter.
