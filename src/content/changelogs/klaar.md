## 30 October 2026
- In Wa, a soft chord now breathes under your tune. It moves with your clears, not with a clock, opens up once most of the board is cleared, and fades to silence when you leave the board alone; your next clear brings it back. The Rhythm switch in Settings turns it off, as it does Hap's beat.
- In Wa, a miss now knocks on low wood, tuned to the chord, instead of a muted note. Hap keeps its muted miss.
- A full clear ends on its own little piece of music: three rolled chords, then the row of numbers lands one by one as a short tune. KLAAR! pops in with the first chord, the numbers burst out with the second, and the "!" nods in with the last. Wa settles warm and slow; Hap stops open, like the start of the next game. The swoosh and the single chord that used to play are gone.
- The result waits until the last note has rung, so a full clear lingers a little longer than other endings.
- A Wa clear with no rewinds and no hints adds one quiet high note, and the "!" nods once more.
- In Hap, a full clear that beats your best on that board shows New best and plays its little nod after the tune.
- The "!" nods a touch further back on its rebound.
- Klaar! has a new sound. Every clear plays one soft mallet note, picked from how you play: quick runs, long thinks and pauses each sound a little different, so a game becomes your own small tune. Wa and Hap each have their own key. The wooden tock on a clear is gone.
- Hap has a wooden beat under your notes, starting when the board goes live. A note that falls just before a beat waits for it; the rest play the moment you clear. Turn the beat off with Rhythm in Settings, right after Sound.
- A miss is a soft, muted mallet tap in the tune's key instead of a wooden thud.
- A board left open (no more moves, time up, or finishing early) ends on one soft note. The low strike at time up is gone.
- A long rewind ticks a few times, each softer, instead of once for every move.
- Hints, New best and the row of numbers after a full clear play in your tune's key.

## 29 October 2026
- Switching between light and dark on the home screen now repaints the daily and mode panels too. They sometimes kept the old colours, for example right after switching back from Hap.
- The example board on the home screen shows its selection the way the game shows an exact match: an orange outline and an orange sum.
- About says plainly what Klaar! does with your data: your save stays on your device, and the game doesn't send your data anywhere.
- A new ending. When a game ends, the buttons step away and the board stays on screen as a picture of how you cleared it. The result slides up at the bottom with Play again. Pull it up for the details, push it down or tap the board to tuck it away, or press Enter to play again. On desktop the result takes the place of the side panel.
- After a full clear, KLAAR! pops in letter by letter and the "!" nods in last. Once it rests, poke a letter, or a number in the row below it: each number plays its own note.
- In Hap, the result now waits until the last note of the row has rung.
- The target number sits right after KLAAR! as part of the word. It jumps, and the "!" nods, every time you clear.
- Rewinding several moves at once: each token grows back over its own spot, instead of the spots going bare first.
- The small example boards on the home screen can really be cleared now, with cleared spots shown the way the game shows them. The Wa and Hap panels in the daily card each wear their own mode's colours.
- KLAAR! on the home screen and above the board is drawn in solid letters now, set a little closer together. Poke a letter and it presses down and springs back; slide along the word and the press follows your finger. The "!" nods.
- Tokens are solid blocks: when you press one, its top sinks onto a base that stays put, instead of the whole token moving. A quick tap on a token presses it and lets go without selecting anything.
- New colours in all four looks. The board sits slightly sunk into the page, the dark boards are lighter so the dark tokens stand out, and cleared spots fill in with cleaner tones.
- The Play button is a brighter orange with a dark label in every look.
- New solid icons on all the buttons.
- Small fixes: on a phone, Go in Enter code is a regular button, as on desktop. A first Hap game no longer shows "Best 0 tokens". "Continue daily" is now "Play daily", and Stats says "Average tokens". On the top row, the sum now sits above the board instead of on your selection.
- The background is one calm colour now. In the dark skin it showed faint horizontal stripes that slid around when a menu opened or the window changed size; they are gone. The page is a touch lighter at the bottom than before.
- Opening a menu no longer flashes dark for a moment before the menu appears, most visible in the light skin. On desktop the menu card stays solid while it rises, instead of fading in see-through.
- Menus now slow down gently as they settle, on phone and desktop alike, and the page behind them dims straight away instead of fading.
- The Klaar! icon sits on one flat colour now, like the game's page.

## 28 October 2026
- Klaar! has its own icon: a small board with 1, 2, 3 and 4, on your browser tab and when you add it to your home screen, instead of the engine's robot.
- While the game loads, a short example board shows how to play: one drag across numbers that add up to the target, and they clear. In my test it loaded no slower than before.
- The example board on the loading page keeps playing while the game starts, instead of freezing halfway, and the first drag begins a little sooner.
- On phones the game now reaches the edges of the screen: no light line on the right or at the bottom, and the band behind the gesture bar takes your skin's colour. The loading bar starts empty instead of draining first.
- Opening a game in Stats no longer throws the list back to the top: it stays where you were, and the opened game is in view.
- Stats is much quicker with a long history. It shows your newest 15 games and a Show more button for the rest, and opening a game now changes only that game, not the whole list. In my test with 200 games saved, Stats opened in about a tenth of a second instead of most of a second.

## 27 October 2026
- Load backup and About no longer cut off their last line on some phone widths.
- On a phone or tablet, Enter code now opens your browser's own text box, so you can paste a code with a long press or from the keyboard, as on any website.
- A Hap code entered on a phone now starts straight into the countdown instead of the Pause card.

## 26 October 2026
- Klaar! now loads with about 4 MB less data on your first visit: the fonts were far bigger than the game needs. Nothing looks different.

## 25 October 2026
- The Hap clock eases back to normal over a moment in the last ten seconds, instead of snapping.
- A "Saved" label that got stuck now turns back by itself.
- A damaged row in your save no longer loses the whole save. The bad game is dropped and the rest stays.
- A game opened from an old link stays in Recent games with its replay, but it no longer counts in your stats, gives a "New best", or enters the Wa average.
- Klaar! draws only when the picture changes, so it is lighter on battery and warmth.

## 22 October 2026
- **About Klaar!** A quiet card, from Settings or the desktop home, with who makes Klaar!, what's new, and links to this devlog and changelog.
- Remove a profile you no longer need. Every profile except the one you are playing as has a Remove, with one confirmation and the offer to save a backup first.
- Switching profiles on a phone now shows the new profile straight away.

## 21 October 2026
- **Sound.** The first sounds are in: one soft mallet, played in one scale, for clears, hints, a full clear and the Hap streak, plus a soft thud for a miss. A new best in Hap gets a small nod. Sound can be switched off in Settings, and the mix is tuned for a phone speaker.
- **Footprints.** Every cleared token leaves a flat mark where it stood, so a cleared board slowly turns into a quiet grid of colour.
- **Tidier menus.** Finish here ends a game early in both Wa and Hap. The Pause card has Share board and Home at the bottom. You can share a board from Stats. Settings is drawn as a gear. Placeholders are gone, except Skins.
- **Scrolling.** Cards scroll from anywhere that is not a button, and list rows scroll when dragged and open when tapped.
- Hap's streak is a little more forgiving.

## 20 October 2026
- **Share board.** The end card shares a link that opens the board you just played, for anyone.
- **Save backup.** Move your progress to another device, or reset it.
- The board teasers on the home screen are livelier.

## 19 October 2026
- **Hap daily.** A second shared board each day, timed. The daily dots read Ready, Played today and Cleared today, with today's best.
- **Profiles and Stats.** Several players can share one device, each with their own games and stats.
- Wa and Hap both play at targets 10, 12 and 15. Hap's timer follows the board size (60, 90 or 120 seconds).
- Type and paste board codes, also from a phone keyboard.
- Lighter on battery and warmth while a board sits idle.
- Fixed: the screen could freeze after going Home.

## 18 October 2026
- **A home screen**, with a switch between Wa (calm) and Hap (fast). The standard game is target 15 on a 9 × 14 board.
- **Wa daily.** One shared board per day, the same for everyone.
- **Hap.** Play against the clock with your own score. A Hap game ends by itself when no move is left.
- Settings, your game choice and finished games are remembered on this device.
- New boards (version 3): groups also form in two directions, not only in lines, and every board has at least one box clear.

## 17 October 2026
- New boards (version 2) with fairer pairs, and three board sizes: Small, Medium and Large.

## 16 October 2026
- **First playable build** in the browser.
- Lighter on the processor while idle, and tidier menu cards.
