## 28 October 2026
- Klaar! has its own icon: a small board with 1, 2, 3 and 4, on your browser tab and when you add it to your home screen, instead of the engine's robot.
- While the game loads, a short example board shows how to play: one drag across numbers that add up to the target, and they clear. In my test it loaded no slower than before.
- Opening a game in Stats no longer throws the list back to the top: it stays where you were, and the opened game is in view.
- Stats is much quicker with a long history. It shows your newest 15 games and a Show more button for the rest, and opening a game now changes only that game, not the whole list. In my test with 200 games saved, Stats opened in about a tenth of a second instead of most of a second.

## 27 October 2026
- Load backup and About no longer cut off their last line on some phone widths.
- On a phone or tablet, Enter code now opens your browser's own text box, so you can paste a code with a long press or from the keyboard, as on any website.
- A Hap code entered on a phone now starts straight into the countdown instead of the Pause card.

## 26 October 2026
- Klaar! now loads with about 4 MB less data on your first visit: the fonts were far bigger than the game needs. Nothing looks different.

## 25 October 2026
- The Hap clock eases back to normal over a moment in the last ten seconds, instead of snapping.
- A "Saved" label that got stuck now turns back by itself.
- A damaged row in your save no longer loses the whole save. The bad game is dropped and the rest stays.
- A game opened from an old link stays in Recent games with its replay, but it no longer counts in your stats, gives a "New best", or enters the Wa average.
- Klaar! draws only when the picture changes, so it is lighter on battery and warmth.

## 22 October 2026
- **About Klaar!** A quiet card, from Settings or the desktop home, with who makes Klaar!, what's new, and links to this devlog and changelog.
- Remove a profile you no longer need. Every profile except the one you are playing as has a Remove, with one confirmation and the offer to save a backup first.
- Switching profiles on a phone now shows the new profile straight away.

## 21 October 2026
- **Sound.** The first sounds are in: one soft mallet, played in one scale, for clears, hints, a full clear and the Hap streak, plus a soft thud for a miss. A new best in Hap gets a small nod. Sound can be switched off in Settings, and the mix is tuned for a phone speaker.
- **Footprints.** Every cleared token leaves a flat mark where it stood, so a cleared board slowly turns into a quiet grid of colour.
- **Tidier menus.** Finish here ends a game early in both Wa and Hap. The Pause card has Share board and Home at the bottom. You can share a board from Stats. Settings is drawn as a gear. Placeholders are gone, except Skins.
- **Scrolling.** Cards scroll from anywhere that is not a button, and list rows scroll when dragged and open when tapped.
- Hap's streak is a little more forgiving.

## 20 October 2026
- **Share board.** The end card shares a link that opens the board you just played, for anyone.
- **Save backup.** Move your progress to another device, or reset it.
- The board teasers on the home screen are livelier.

## 19 October 2026
- **Hap daily.** A second shared board each day, timed. The daily dots read Ready, Played today and Cleared today, with today's best.
- **Profiles and Stats.** Several players can share one device, each with their own games and stats.
- Wa and Hap both play at targets 10, 12 and 15. Hap's timer follows the board size (60, 90 or 120 seconds).
- Type and paste board codes, also from a phone keyboard.
- Lighter on battery and warmth while a board sits idle.
- Fixed: the screen could freeze after going Home.

## 18 October 2026
- **A home screen**, with a switch between Wa (calm) and Hap (fast). The standard game is target 15 on a 9 × 14 board.
- **Wa daily.** One shared board per day, the same for everyone.
- **Hap.** Play against the clock with your own score. A Hap game ends by itself when no move is left.
- Settings, your game choice and finished games are remembered on this device.
- New boards (version 3): groups also form in two directions, not only in lines, and every board has at least one box clear.

## 17 October 2026
- New boards (version 2) with fairer pairs, and three board sizes: Small, Medium and Large.

## 16 October 2026
- **First playable build** in the browser.
- Lighter on the processor while idle, and tidier menu cards.
